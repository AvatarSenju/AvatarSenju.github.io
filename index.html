01 / About

Curiosity first.
Then, under the hood.

I’m a Software Engineer at AMD, working across networking firmware, Linux device drivers, and userspace systems. I connect platform behavior with the tools engineers use to configure, validate, and troubleshoot it.

My work spans programmable packet processing, embedded RTOS simulation, kernel-to-userspace APIs, and GPU networking infrastructure. I also build diagnostics, monitoring pipelines, and reproducible deployment and test workflows.

In personal projects, I bring that systems mindset to AI orchestration and developer tools: Python backends, persistent task workflows, and browser interfaces that keep a person in the loop.

My kind of rabbit hole? Following a surprising behavior across the stack until it makes sense. The satisfying part is turning that “why?” into a fix, and a test that catches it next time.
